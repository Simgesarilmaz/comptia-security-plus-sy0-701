# Audits and Assessments

## Overview

### Audit

An audit is a systematic evaluation of an organization's:

- Information systems
- Applications
- Security controls
- Operational processes

Audits verify whether security controls are operating as intended.

---

### Assessment

An assessment is a detailed analysis performed to identify:

- Vulnerabilities
- Risks
- Security weaknesses
- Control gaps

Assessments help organizations improve their security posture before incidents occur.

---

## Primary Objectives

Audits and assessments are performed to:

- Validate security controls
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Identify vulnerabilities and risks
- Support continuous improvement
- Improve overall security posture

---

## Types of Audits

| Audit Type | Description | Example |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| **Internal Audit** | Conducted by internal teams within the organization | Reviewing data protection policies |
| **External Audit** | Conducted by independent third-party auditors | PCI DSS compliance audit |

---

Audits help organizations:

### Identify Security Gaps

## Types of Assessments

| Assessment Type | Purpose |
|-----------------|---------|
| **Risk Assessment** | Evaluates potential threats, likelihood, and business impact |
| **Vulnerability Assessment** | Identifies known system vulnerabilities |
| **Threat Assessment** | Analyzes threats that may affect the organization |

---

## When Assessments Are Performed

Assessments are commonly performed:

- Before deploying new systems
- Before major infrastructure changes
- After security incidents
- During compliance reviews
- During mergers or acquisitions

---

## Internal Audits and Assessments

### Internal Audit

Internal audits are performed by the organization's internal teams.

### Common Areas Reviewed

- Network security
- Access controls
- Incident response
- Data protection
- Configuration management
- Security awareness

---

## Internal Audit Process

### 1. Review Policies and Procedures

Evaluate whether governance documentation is current and effective.

---

### 2. Review Access Controls

Verify:

- User permissions
- Role assignments
- Privileged access

---

### 3. Test Security Controls

Examples:

- Firewall rules
- Authentication systems
- Logging mechanisms
- Backup processes

---

### 4. Document Findings

Audit findings should include:

- Identified weaknesses
- Risk ratings
- Recommended improvements

---

## Audit Committee

An audit committee is a governance body responsible for oversight.

### Responsibilities

- Review internal and external audit results
- Monitor regulatory compliance
- Evaluate internal controls
- Review financial and operational reporting
- Ensure risk management effectiveness

### Typical Members

Audit committees often include:

- Board members
- Senior executives
- Compliance officers
- Risk management leaders

---

## Important Exam Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Audit** | Verifies whether controls are working correctly |
| **Assessment** | Identifies risks, vulnerabilities, or threats |
| **Internal Audit** | Performed by internal teams |
| **External Audit** | Performed by independent third parties |

---

## Summary

Audits focus on:

- Validation
- Compliance
- Control effectiveness

Assessments focus on:

- Risk identification
- Vulnerability discovery
- Security improvement

Together, they help organizations maintain governance, compliance, and security maturity.
